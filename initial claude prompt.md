In this repo, I had made an attempt at writing a sci-fi novel, but it didn't really work out. I'd like to try again though and use this story as the foundation for the lore in the flutter based video game I want to create. 
The game I want to create is a rougelike deck building auto-battler. The game is split into 3 stages, with the galactic map randomly generated each time. 
The player character is a ship captain and can be any of the any of the species described in 'The sentient species of the milky way.md' except for humans, hellborn, and Havi. 
Each species has their own starting ship, which have different stats. One of the most important stats is human recources (HR), this is the number of humans on your ship and how much the humans on your ship like you. Humans like certain species and certain species like humans. Certain evens and resources will cause your captain and ship to veer more towards human culture while others cause humans to become further ingrained in their captain's culture. HR is presented by the game as an unimportant stat where the only thing that matters is to have your humans happy enough that they won't destroy the ship to kill the captain, but the humans play a very important role in the story and having a good relationship whith the humans onboard and keeping a large amount of them will cause hidden effects on the story's progression and change the odds of certain random events.
The captain's job is to find a crew, find a ship, and make enough money to make ends meet. The galaxy's story will play around as the captain takes their turns. The captain can have events happen, accept various missions, buy and sell resources, and build their deck at each node. Travel between nodes that are connected through gateways always ends the player's turn and the next turn starts in the next node, but certain events can occur where the captain can be intentionally or unintentionally brought into the hell dimension while travelling through a gateway pipe. entering hell outside a pipe is extremely dangerous but can yield extreme rewards. Interaction with hell in only through events and combat. When the captain manages to escape hell, they will exit into a random gateway. Entering hell in the first act will only allow the player to exit in a gateway that's connected to the system in the first act or die.
The layout of the galaxy is always randomized with a few elements being deterministic. 
The game plays out in three acts.

Act one: The center, as described in 'The Center.md' is always the main hub in the first act with 3 active gateways and one inactive gateway that leads to tre-tre-trene. 
The node network connected to the center always includes Ocha station, Bhrun-gai, Ghor-Dum, and Kepler.
Ghor-dum always has two gateways: Bhrun-gai and one random.
Bhrun-Gai always has two gateways: Ghor-dum and Kepler.
Kepler always has only one gateway: Bhrun-Gai.
The following systems exist in the first act but are only reachable by sublight travel from an adjacent node:
- Neo Terra is always in the space adjacent to Ocha station, but is not revaled until a certain event happens.
- Ur-Gor is always adjacent to Ghor-Dum and is revaled at the start.

Act two: The gateway getween the center and tre-tre-trene is restored and efforts begin to mount in order to reunite the rest of the galaxy and restore the gateway network to its original state.
The node network connected to tre-tre-trene always includes Úlamora. Úlaval is in an adjacent node to Úlamora and only reachable through sublight travel.

Act three: Any gateway is restored that connects Kyndari to the node network.
This is the final act. As soon as Kyndari is discovered, it will be revealed that there is an active gateway from Kyndari to Sol. Sol is always adjacent to Kepler and can be traversed in sublight travel. This reveals the full extent of the humans in showing that they've managed to restore a gateway without the help of the Havi, Unfortunates or Tern. Depending on how events have played out and how the captain has used their small but significant political power, there are three endgames.
- Code Blue: The humans are accepted by the Republic and have become meaningful allies to them. This can only be used when the humans have been granted a planet to settle on.
- Code Red: The Republic has decided to eliminate or enslave the humans.
- Code Yellow: The humans are fighting an existential threat that is not the Republic.
