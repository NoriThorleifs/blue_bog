There are two dimensions: our dimension is called the vaccuum dimension because it is large and mostly consists of vaccuum. 
The other dimension is referred by different names for each language, but each of them has a translation or cultural signifigance that roughly translates to "The bad place" or "Hell".
Hell is a more compact mirror to our dimension. Distances crossed in Hell correspond to much greater distances in the Vaccuum. The time saved by going the route depends on the time dilation in that particular route, but the time it would take to make the same journey will always be greater. The time it takes to cross the entire width of the milky way through hell at cruising speed is about 104 years, as experienced by the people on the ship.
Cruising speed is defined by the galactic standard of units and a multiple of three to fit the ternary system, will need to define the system later, but the speed is about 55000 Km/s, give or take 10000 km/s.
Crossing between the dimensions requires iterstellar gates. These gates are ancient relics that are preserved with care, with only the Tern, Unfortunates and Havi knowing how they work. Each interstellar gate exists simultaniously in the same point in both the vaccuum and hell. Each gate has an energy barrier in hell that extends to the gate that it is paired to. The denizens of hell are sometimes able to briefly pierce through the barrier, but not for long. During the gatecrash, the most powerful entities in hell banded together to break down a few of the barriers, causing an apocalypse and the collapse of most of the gate system.
The gates orbit around the center of the galaxy in tandem with the stars they are anchored to. Multiple gates can be arranged around a single star, but this is limited to 4 or 5 before they need to be spaced much further apart. 4 gates need to be spaced evenly around a star in the distance equivalent to about 1 AU, otherwise they will overlap in hell. 
When a ship (or any object) approaches a gate from either dimension, the gate will begin to influence said object and gradually bring it into the other dimension. This effect is described as fading between. The fade occurs at the same time regardless of entry speed, but intergalactic laws dictate that all ships adhere to the predefined cruising speed and must speed up or slow down to that spped prior to entry. The gates are circular, but all traffic is split into thirds. lower right goes in, lower right goes out. The top third is split into thirds again with the separation being flipped horizonally so that there is one third that is closest to the middle which is reserved for emergency traffic. The top left and right of the remaining thirds are for drone traffic in and out. The drones are allowed to operate at much higher speeds.
The entire galaxy in the hell dimension has its space filled by an alcoholic substance and heavyer elements are far more abundant. Chemistry and physics do not work the same as in the vaccuum. Life exists in a freakish and monstrous collection of metallic flesh and alcoholic blood.
Most beings in Hell are not intelligent, but some larger beings can become sentient.
Time passes anywhere between 10X faster to passing in reverse at 3.2X speed. The exact mechanism of time
in Hell is not fully understood, but it is known through experimentation that it is deterministic and dependant on the path and speed taken through Hell. This time dilation
can only be measured by taking an analog clock onboard a ship and comparing how much time it gained or lost compared to an identical clock. Humans are the only species crazy enough to attempt to use the negative time passing to speed up logistics, but a successful run can have a parcel shipped to its destination before it is ordered. The only way to explore hell or take any path other than what is outlined by the barrier is to be inside a gateway pipe and have a demon briefly pierce the barrier and then squeeze the ship through that piercing. There is only one route that humans are able to do this regularly in, and that's because there's a larger demon that's seemingly using the barrier pipe as a chew toy. Thankfully this demon does not have a mouth, it's just a bunch of teeth.
