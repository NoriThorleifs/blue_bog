In the ternary system, all units come in only three forms:
1. Threes
2. Thirds
3. Powers of three

If you have three items, they must each contribute to the whole. It's okay if they're different from each-other, but there can't be one that doesn't contribute anything that the other two do not.

If you have thirds, they must form a whole. The only way to form their whole must be with only these three thirds.

If you have a lot of the same thing, it is expressed as the lowest power of three equal to or less than the number plus the amount of threes needed to reach it. Numbers that are one or two from being a power of three cannot be formed.
