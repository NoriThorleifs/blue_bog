The Bhrun were preparing for the political endgame that they would try to appease the Gor when the Humans suddenly appeared out of the gateway from Pax Morra. The Humans explained that if the Gor took over the system, they would be next on the chopping block. 

The Human armada went into the Bhrun-Gor gateway to make one final stand. 
To everyone's surprise, the Progenitus went along with the Human armada.
None to the Gor armada made it through.

Nobody can explain how they won, but scattered and jammed transmissions say that that the Humans managed to bring down the pipe to destroy the fleet without the Progenitus being affected.

The Progenitus then returned to Kepler system without a word.
