A Havi scientist whose consciousness was given to a supercomputer inside the high-gravity world that would become Neo Terra, built as a doomsday vault of information (see the [[outline]]). An uploaded mind rather than a living Havi, so it's "it".

## Names

Before the Neo Terran humans find out what it is, they don't know it as Shining-head. They call it **"The Entity"** or **"Radio Host"**: a voice under their capital that talks to them over the radio. It's only revealed as Shining-head later.

## What it does

- Cut off by the gatecrash, it took control of the Consumers, and went mad from loneliness. Its Consumer strikes on the Republic were a cry for help and for attention.
- Its main station lies deep under the surface, and the humans' capital is built on top of it. When the humans tried digging down to it, it threatened to blow up the planet's core.
