Notes and concepts on what each species looks like, for the art and for any UI that shows them. Who they are lives in [[The sentient species of the milky way]]; this note is only about how they look.

Each species has three parts:
- **Settled:** what I've decided. This is the brief for an artist.
- **Concept art:** the placeholder mockups from `tool/generate_icon_concepts.py`, made as app icon candidates. They are proof-of-concept placeholders until a real artist takes over.
- **Open:** what's still undecided.

## Gor

**Settled**
- Gangly and muscular.
- Four legs carry a lower body that sits at a 45° angle when standing.
- The lower body meets the upper body at a mobile joint. The upper body is upright when standing.
- Two arms on each side, four in all.

**Concept art:** a silhouette against a sunset over Ur-Gor's hills, holding a spear. The body plan reads, but the head, the spear and the sunset were the mockup's choices, not mine.

![[concept_art/icon_5_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- The head and face.
- Skin, colouring, and any armour or clothes.
- How big they are next to the other species.

## Bhrun

**Settled**
- Covered in grey, rock-like plates.
- A massive, beetle-like body on four massive legs.
- Complex plates on the face, which move.

**Concept art:** head-on, with a dome of cracked rock plates, legs in two plated segments, and a face of overlapping plates with eyes deep between them.

![[concept_art/icon_7_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- I originally imagined them as silicon-based, but I'm no longer sure.
- What the face plates do when they move: eating, talking, expression?

## Ál

**Settled**
- Black, because they evolved in a low-light environment: the deep sea of Úlamora.
- Like the Sentinel machines from *The Matrix* films, but organic and with thicker tentacles.

**Concept art:** a black body rising with thick tentacles trailing behind it. A big pale eye with an octopus's bar pupil, four smaller eyes round it, and a sheen from faint light far above. Rows of glowing spots, as if they make their own light. "Decently close" to how I picture them.

![[concept_art/icon_6_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- Whether they really make their own light, or the glowing spots were just the mockup's way of showing a black creature in the dark.
- How many eyes they have, and what they look like out of the water.

## Tern

**Settled**
- Bodies made of nanobots, which can merge with other Tern or split. They usually keep to about the size of the average minor species.

**Concept art:** a glowing lattice of nanobots in a triangle, coming apart at the edges, with a core of three. This is right.

![[concept_art/icon_4_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- What a single Tern looks like at its everyday size.

## Unfortunates

**Settled**
- Immortal machines. They come in all sorts of shapes and sizes.
- Smooth lines and curved features, with white metal or platinum exteriors. Not rough, and not rusted tin buckets.
- Elvish-inspired, but not elves. No elf ears.
- A shadow of their former selves since the gatecrash.
- The Eldest is thousands of years old.

**Concept art:** one of them, front on: a long face of polished white metal narrowing to a fine chin, narrow glowing eyes, a thin gold circlet, and a crack running down one cheek. A gate glows faintly behind. (An earlier version had elf-like fins, and an earlier one still showed rusted hulks; both were wrong.)

![[concept_art/icon_8_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- How the gatecrash shows on them: wear, damage, missing parts?
- What the Eldest looks like, and whether age shows on an immortal machine.

## Hellborn

**Settled**
- A dark, horned war helm with burning eye slits fits them. It was first drawn for the Gor, and it isn't the Gor.

![[concept_art/icon_9_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**

## Demons

**Settled**
- Lovecraftian monsters. "Demon" isn't one species but every native of Hell, so they come in all shapes and sizes.
- The Chewer, from the game's Hell events: "something with no mouth and a great many teeth".

**Concept art:** a shapeless, veined body with too many slit-pupilled eyes, tentacles, and a vertical mouth that's mostly teeth.

![[concept_art/icon_10_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- Satan.

## Havi

**Settled**
- Ethereal and godlike, and near invincible.
- They can morph into whatever shape they need, but mostly appear as an amoeba-like cloud of golden smoke and gel.
- Gender doesn't really apply to the Havi. The Overseer is they/them. Shining-head is "it", being an uploaded mind rather than a living Havi. Don't sweat a god's pronouns.

**Concept art:** a lobed body of glowing golden gel with veins of light inside, golden smoke curling away from it, and droplets of gel drifting off.

![[concept_art/icon_11_ai_generated.png|240]]

**Open**
- Whether individual Havi look different from each other: the Overseer, the Mourner, Shining-head.

## Humans

**Settled**
- Just humans.
- Humans give genders to things that don't have them: Mister Jay is "he" to them, though he's a nanobot swarm.
- Their icon would be something along the lines of the Vitruvian Man.
- Humans who grow up in low gravity grow taller and lankier. The story's humans haven't been out of the Promethius long enough for that: its centrifuge habitat pulls 1 G. (The Hellborn, changed by millennia in Hell, are the exception.)

**Concept art:** none yet.
