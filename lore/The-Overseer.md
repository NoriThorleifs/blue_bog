This Havi got the short end of the stick in its entire existence. Never respected by its peers and never entrusted to do anything of importance. After the construction of the new space station at an important 4-way junction that became the [center for intergalactic tourism](The%20Center), the other Havi assigned this runt of the litter to be its overseer so that they could forget about it. The center was built with a special chamber that serves as its tomb. A superior being trapped inside a complex where the minor species come to travel to new places. 
When the gatecrash occurred, the overseer was the only Havi remaining in the reduced network that became the republic.
The overseer ironically became the most important Havi in the universe for these minor species.

Due to the way it was treated by its peers, the overseer is not the least bit excited about reopening the gates that are able to be opened. Every time the minor species consider a solution to open a gateway, the overseer secretly makes sure that it is put to a vote, just so that it can veto the decision and further delay. It hides behind vague arguments that the solution proposed wouldn't work, but it has no idea how the gates even work because it never learned engineering.
