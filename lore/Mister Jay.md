Tjí-Li-La-Jay, A.K.A. Mister Jay: a Tern who forms a strong bond with the humans, and the one who reopens the gateway between the Center and Træ Træ Tene. This starts act 2. A draft.

## The coffin

1. **Wrong warping.** The humans show Mister Jay wrong warping (see the [[outline]]). He gets an idea. He secretly encourages them to keep experimenting, and helps them keep it secret.
2. **The plan.** The humans will send a tiny, torpedo-shaped ship carrying one human through one of the gateways, on a wrong-warp path that should, in theory, come out at Træ Træ Tene. The ship is known as "the coffin".
3. **A piece of himself.** Mister Jay splits off a small part of himself to ride in the coffin, carrying his memories of what humans are and of what he's planning. When the Tern at Træ Træ Tene discover those memories, they'll know to activate the gate on their side at one exact moment. They time it by measuring and counting the cycles of a pulsar between the two gates.
4. **The gate opens.** Mister Jay activates the gate at the Center at that moment, and every species freaks out. Only the humans were in on the plan, and he'd had no way of confirming whether the wrong warp worked.
5. **What nobody knows.** It did work, but not on its own. The [[The sentient species of the milky way|Hellborn]] stepped in, got the coffin to the right destination, and phased it in through the gate. The pilot died along the way, but Mister Jay's memories made it through.

## In the game

The coffin is one of three routes into act 2 (see `Game design - map and story.md`). When the news of the plans for the dead gateway reaches the captain, they can help Mister Jay and the humans, and the gate opens this way. If they stay out of it, it can still happen this way, and does more often the more the humans aboard trust the captain. Either way the Hellborn's part stays secret; the captain's humans only hint that someone on the other side caught the coffin.
