As she unlocked her grandfather's seal at the back and he immediately ripped it off, a thought occurred to her that she couldn't help but say out loud.

"How come you need someone else to take it off with a key?"

"It's a safety thing, don't think about it too much" he replied quickly, as he'd anticipated the question.

As she left to join her mother for dinner, he curled up in his chair to a sitting fetal position. It was the brace position that he instinctively assumed when he's terrified. He would never let another person see him like this. Twitching rythmically and sobbing as quietly as he could, covering his ears for explosions that he hadn't heard in 40 years but still haunt him to this day.
