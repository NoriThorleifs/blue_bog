This system used to be the main hub of all trade. This was until the gatecrash. 
The system contained the cradle world of the Unfortunates.
In the first phase of the gatecrash, the demons were streaming in from the portal to Zirmai.

After the gatecrash, the Kyndari system was nothing but an ancient ruin left behind by the demons after being consumed and abandoned.

XX Years later, the Humans discovered the system and figured out that they were the first people to reach their world and mess with their politics.

The Humans named them "The Unfortunates" and nobody objected this name. Even the unfortunates accepted this noun as their official name until the end of time. It was only fitting, based on the things they did for the Havi.
