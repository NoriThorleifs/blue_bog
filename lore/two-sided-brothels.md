Two-sided brothels are the result of a complicated legal loophole. 
Humans are categorized as having inferior intelligence to the Ál and therefore cannot consent to having sex with them.
Because the Ál want to stick their tentacles into the warm and squishy bodies of the Humans, and the Humans want to get their insides reamed, a solution had to be found.
Due to multiple archaic rules in the galaxy, a loophole was found:
- In all neutral stations in the galaxy: Sale of prostitution is legal as long as the entity selling themselves is intelligent enough to know what they're doing. 
- In the entire galaxy: If prostitution is purchased with the contract of having both the facilitator and prostitute are bound to silence, the contract must be upheld and neither party can be forced to testify against the other in court. The purchaser is therefore expected to hide their identity and the facilitator must not record the interaction.
With these laws combined, a solution is found.
The Humans are not recognized as intelligent enough to consent to sex with a higher being, but they are intelligent enough to know what they're doing when they prostitute themselves with the risk of bodily harm and death. 
If both the Human and Ál in the exchange are both prostituting themselves and purchasing a prostitute to have sex with them at the same time with the contract to maintain secrecy on both sides, the loophole is formed.
The two-sided brothels work by having two entrances, one is intended for Áls and the other for Humans. Both the Ál and the Human negotiate how much they're paying for a prostitute and then negotiate the price for selling their own bodies. The Ál then enters a water tank with a hole on the side that's barely small enough so that an adult human couldn't squeeze through. At the same time, the Human enters on the other side of the wall. The Human enters an inclined pool that is only waist deep at the deepest end. The pool has a hole on the deep end that leads to the Ál.
The Ál is then both getting paid to put its tentacles through the hole and letting whatever's on the other side do whatever it wants with it, while at the same time being paid to use its tentacles to do whatever the party on the other side commands it to do. The brothel takes a small cut of each exchange, while encouraging both parties to sell themselves for increasing amounts.
