Havi:
Godlike rulers of the universe. Their origin and method of reproduction is a mystery. 
These beings have dominated the galaxy for more than a millennia. These beings originate in Hell, but the minor races don't know that.
notable characters:
- [The Overseer](The-Overseer.md)
- [The Mourner](The-Mourner.md)
- Shining Head

Unfortunates:
These machine beings originate in the Kyndari system. Nobody knows what happened to the biological beings that made them, people aren't sure if it was the Havi who made them or if they made modifications to the machines to uplift them. The Unfortunates are constantly shrouded in mystery. They are said to have assisted in building the gateways and posses the technology to create new gates, but they adamantly refuse to admit how much they know.
notable characters:
- The Eldest

Tern:
These mechanical beings were made by the Havi to optimize their empire. The main world dedicated to producing the Tern is Kyberon. The Tern functioned as the distributed computer processing power of the empire. After the gatecrash, the Tern are the glue keeping the republic together. There was at least one Tern stationed in each of the star systems in the old Havi empire.
The Tern are more powerful than the Unfortunates in every way, but they are extremely expensive to produce. The Tern bodies are composed of nanobots that are capable of self replication if they have access to the right materials.
They have very little concept of self identity and will form a hivemind with other Terns that are within radio range. They can also merge or split their bodies depending on what their situation requires, but will usually keep themselves in a size akin to the average size of the minor species.
Notable characters:
- Tjí-Li-La-Jay A.K.A Mister Jay

Bhrun:
Slow, Passive, Unimportant.
Literally nobody gives a fuck about this species and they're too dumb to figure it out.
The Gor want to subjugate them to compensate for their small dick syndrome from being an aggressive race that hasn't conquered anything. 
The Humans protect the Bhrun because they know the Humans are next on the chopping block if the Gor take over Bhrun-Gai. The Bhrun take the Humans for their word and believe it's because they have a positive relationship from their extremely limited and one-sided trade. Everyone knows the Bhrun would never call the Humans their friends and would never lift a finger to protect them.
Notable characters:
- Ben Buru’hyrem’him Burhuge A.K.A Ben
- Bar'ba'ra Ara Bar'ara'ara'ba Bara'rababara A.K.A. Barbara
 
Ál:
Curious, Horny, Aquatic.
Originating from the world of Úlamora, these tentacle-laden beings have survived by poking and prodding their way through everything. 
They managed to develop their sub-light travel technology well enough to colonize a nearby system that they were fortunate enough to find. The world Úlaval is almost exactly like their home world and only 156 light years away. 
notable characters:
- Ái Á á Á á á á A.K.A. Owie

Humans:
Industrious, friendly, aggressive.
These mysterious beings originated in the solar system. The coordinates of the home world was intentionally scrubbed from their databases. The Humans claim it was to stop themselves from trying to go back home to their doomed system with a dying star, but most species agree that's bullshit. They're hiding something, we just don't know what.
Notable characters:
- Odin the Hellacious
- Yidun the Giantess
- General Grönigen

Hellborn:
Demonic, Patient, Scheming.
These are the humans that have been in hell for millennia, resulting in rapid mutations that have made them adapted to the otherworldly conditions by Darwinian evolution. Their point of origin is the portal to the solar system, in the realm of the demon lord Satan.

Demons:
The term "Demon" is not a single species, but a catch-all term for all natural denizens of Hell. They come in all shapes and sizes.
