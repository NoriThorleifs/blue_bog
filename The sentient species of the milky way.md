Havi:
Godlike rulers of the universe. Their origin and method of reproduction is a mystery. 
These beings have dominated the galaxy for more than a millennia. These beings originate in Hell, but the minor races don't know that.

Unfortunates:
These machine beings originate in the Kyndari system. Nobody knows what happened to the biological beings that made them, people aren't sure if it was the Havi who made them or if they made modifications to the machines to uplift them. The Unfortunates are constantly shrouded in mystery. They are said to have assisted in building the gateways and posses the technology to create new gates, but they adamantly refuse to admit how much they know.

Tern:
These mechanical beings were made by the Havi to optimize their empire. The main world dedicated to producing the Tern is Kyberon. The Tern functioned as the distributed computer processing power of the empire. After the gatecrash, the Tern are the glue keeping the republic together. There was at least one Tern stationed in each of the star systems in the old Havi empire.
The Tern are more powerful than the Unfortunates in every way, but they are extremely expensive to produce. The Tern bodies are composed of nanobots that are capable of self replication if they have access to the right materials.

Bhrun:
Stupid, Passive, Unimportant.
Literally nobody gives a fuck about this species and they're too dumb to figure it out.
The Gor want to subjugate them to compensate for their small dick syndrome from being an aggressive race that hasn't conquered anything. 
The Humans protect the Bhrun because they know the Humans are next on the chopping block if the Gor take over Bhrun-Gai. The Bhrun take the Humans for their word and believe it's because they have a positive relationship from their extremely limited and one-sided trade. Everyone knows the Bhrun would never call the Humans their friends and would never lift a finger to protect them.
